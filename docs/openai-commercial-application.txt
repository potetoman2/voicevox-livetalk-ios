Submitted product description (summary):
LiveTalk is an iPhone voice conversation app for individual Japanese-speaking users.
On-device Japanese speech recognition and VOICEVOX speech synthesis run without a PC.
After explicit consent, recognized text and recent context are sent to OpenAI; streamed
Responses API text is synthesized in short segments. Web search can return clickable
citations. Users can select account-provided models and supported reasoning levels.
Authentication tokens are kept in Keychain, and conversation text is not persisted in
settings or diagnostics. Users can withdraw consent and delete local registrations.
The app will be free with non-personalized settings-page banner ads and an optional
JPY 980 one-time, non-consumable StoreKit purchase that removes ads. All conversation
features remain identical. There is no business plan or recurring subscription.
Questions, audio, account identifiers and citations are not supplied to advertising.
Ads are suppressed during conversation, purchases, background and any future CarPlay.
We request commercial approval for BOTH the ad-supported free app and the ad-removal
purchase, and guidance on production OAuth registration, ChatGPT plan usage, permitted
platforms, account/model/tool support, data-processing terms and App Store review access.
The public-source local evaluation connection is not claimed as commercial approval.
CarPlay will not be marketed or enabled until Apple approves the relevant entitlement.
